Privacy policy

Floor General: Basketball Sim for iPhone (Android to follow) · Last updated 10 November 2026

Floor General collects anonymous usage statistics and nothing that identifies you. It has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. It counts things like “a game was played” or “a season ended 22–8”, so we can see which parts of the game work and which do not. One switch in Settings turns that off. Your career itself is saved on your own phone and the game plays fully offline.

Who we are

Floor General is made and published by Vibalyze OÜ, Tallinn, Estonia, which is the controller of the data described here. You can reach us at vibalyze@zohomail.eu.

What we collect

Anonymous usage statistics

While you play, the app records short events about the game and sends them to our own server: for example that a career started (and with which build), that a game was played (the level, whether it was won, the score margin, your points, the game plan picked), which choice was taken on a story card, how a season ended, which awards were won, and when the app was opened and for how long. Each event carries only numbers, yes/no values and the game’s own fixed ids. It never contains the name you typed for your player, any team or character name, or anything else you wrote.

With the events we send the app version, the operating system and its version, the language of the app, your device’s time-zone offset, and a random install id. The install id is created by the app, is not derived from your device or your Apple or Google account, is replaced after 13 months, and is deleted with the app. The server sees your connection’s IP address only to answer the request and to derive the country; it never stores or logs the address.

We use these statistics only to understand and improve the game (for example retention, session length, and whether a decision is ever taken). We do not sell them, share them, combine them with other data, or use them for advertising, and they are not linked to you. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in improving the game (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)); you can object at any time with the switch below.

Turning it off: on the start screen tap the gear, then switch off Share stats. The app then deletes the events still waiting on the phone and sends nothing more.

Apple Search Ads attribution (iPhone)

On the first launch the app asks iOS for an Apple attribution token (Apple’s AdServices framework) and sends it once, with the install id, to our server, which asks Apple whether the install came from one of our Apple Search Ads campaigns. This uses no advertising identifier, is not tracking under Apple’s rules, and needs no tracking prompt. It is not sent when Share stats is off.

“Write to us”

If you send us a message from Settings › Write to us, we receive the message, the kind you picked (bug, idea or other), the email address if you chose to give one, and the same app details and install id as above. We use it only to read and answer you. It works even with Share stats off, because you chose to send it.

Error reports

If the app hits a programming error, it sends an anonymous error report with the statistics: the kind of error, where in the app’s code it happened (file names, function names and line numbers), whether the app had to stop, and the same app details and install id. It never includes the error’s message or anything you typed. We use it only to find and fix bugs. It is off when Share stats is off.

What we never collect

No advertising identifier (IDFA or Android advertising id), no device serial or vendor id, no contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera, no account, and no purchase data (there are no purchases). The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDK from another company. Crashes of the app itself (outside its own code) are reported by Apple and Google to us only if you have chosen to share them with developers in your phone’s settings.

Where it is stored and for how long

Our server runs on Cloudflare (a processor acting on our instructions), with the database stored in the European Union. Raw events and the attribution record are kept for 120 days; after that only daily totals remain, which describe no single install (kept 25 months). Messages are kept for up to 24 months.

What the app stores on your device

One file, career.json, inside the app’s own documents folder: a seed (one number the game uses to decide what happens), the name you typed for your player, the list of actions you took, and a small fingerprint the game uses to check the file still matches its own rules. The game is deterministic, so instead of saving the state of your career it saves the instructions for rebuilding it. The name you type is treated as a character name; it never leaves the device.

Small companions: hall_of_fame.json (a one-line summary of each finished career), settings.json (haptics on or off), review.json (when the rating prompt was last asked), onboarding.json (that your first player was built), and the statistics queue and install id in the app’s support folder (events waiting to be sent).

Device backups

Because the save lives in the app’s documents folder, it is picked up by your phone’s normal backup (device or iCloud backup on iOS, Android Auto Backup on Android), so reinstalling the app does not delete a career in progress. Those backups are handled by Apple and Google under their own privacy policies. We cannot see them.

The rating prompt

At a few high moments — a first pro contract, a first title, a first MVP — the game may ask the operating system to show its standard “rate this app” sheet. That sheet is drawn and run by Apple or Google, not by us; a rating or review goes to the store under the store’s own privacy policy.

Accounts, online play, advertising, purchases

There are none. No login, no chat, no cloud save, no leaderboard, no ads and nothing to buy. The other characters in the game are invented by the game. The entire game works with the phone in aeroplane mode; statistics simply wait until there is a connection, or are dropped after a week.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Floor General deletes everything on the phone, including the install id. To have the statistics or messages already sent erased from our server, write to vibalyze@zohomail.eu; we erase everything held for your install. Because the data is not linked to you, we may ask you to send a message from Write to us first, so we can find your install id.

Children

Floor General is intended for players aged 12 and over and is not directed at young children. The statistics are anonymous and the same for every age, nothing a player types into the game leaves the device, and there are no ads, purchases, chat or profiles. If you believe a child has sent us a message with personal information, write to us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete and export personal data about you, to object to processing based on legitimate interest, and to complain to a data-protection authority (in Estonia, the Andmekaitse Inspektsioon, or the authority where you live). Write to vibalyze@zohomail.eu.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts doing something this page does not describe — ads, purchases, cloud save — this page is updated first, with a new date at the top, and the store privacy forms are updated with it. We will not quietly add collection under an old policy.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or anything on this page: vibalyze@zohomail.eu.
