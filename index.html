FLOOR GENERAL

A whole basketball career. No energy, no waiting. Games you can read.

What it is

A text-based simulator of a basketball career. You start as a high school player and climb through college to the pros, and if it all goes right, to a legacy people still talk about. When the playing days end, the front office is waiting.

How it works

  • Games you can read. Every game is told as text play-by-play, with a few key decisions along the way that change how it goes.
  • High school to legend. One career, from your first varsity season through college and the pros to how you are remembered.
  • The front office. A GM layer lets you run a team once you are done playing for one.
  • Your choices, your career. Every career is built from a seed and the choices you make. Same seed and same choices, same career.
  • Your legends. Finished careers stay listed on your phone, with how each one ended.

All fiction

Every player, coach, team, school, conference and league in Floor General is invented. No real athlete or organisation appears in the game, and it is not affiliated with or endorsed by any league, association or governing body.

What it does not do

No ads. No in-app purchases. No timers or energy. No accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no networking code at all — it works in aeroplane mode. Your career is a small file on your own phone. See the privacy policy, which is short because there is nothing to describe.
